Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-05. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Who we are

Shear Style by Amanda is a hair salon business operated by Amanda at Surface Studio, 1255 Rosecrans Street, San Diego, CA 92106. You can reach us at (619) 857-1545 or shearstylebyamanda@gmail.com.

Information this website collects

This website does not have accounts, contact forms, or newsletter sign-ups, and it does not ask you for personal information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability.

If we add privacy-friendly analytics, we will use a cookieless service that reports overall visit counts without identifying you, and we will update this page.

Booking and gift cards (Vagaro)

When you tap “Book Appointment” or “Gift Cards,” you leave this website and go to Vagaro, a third-party booking and payments platform. Information you enter there (such as your name, phone, email, and payment details) is collected and handled by Vagaro under its own privacy policy and terms. Please review Vagaro’s policies on their website.

Embedded maps and links

Our location map is provided by Google Maps. When the map loads, Google may collect information under Google’s privacy policy. Links to Instagram and other sites are governed by those sites’ policies.

Phone and email

If you call or email us, we use your information only to respond to you and manage your appointment. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and California rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you from calls or emails, or ask us to delete it, by contacting shearstylebyamanda@gmail.com. Requests about your Vagaro account should be made through Vagaro.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
