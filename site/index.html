FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?

Tap Book Appointment anywhere on this site to see open times and book online through Vagaro. You can also call (619) 857-1545.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome. Booking ahead online is the surest way to get the day and time you want.

Where is the salon?

Amanda works inside Surface Studio at 1255 Rosecrans Street, San Diego, CA 92106, on Rosecrans in Point Loma.

What’s the difference between New Guest and Returning services?

The menu is organized for first-time guests and returning guests. If this is your first visit with Amanda, choose from the New Guest services. A few prices differ between the two menus, so check the full service menu before you book.

Do you offer consultations?

Yes. A consultation is listed at no charge in both the new-guest haircut and new-guest color menus. It’s a good first step if you’re planning a bigger change.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please allow 48 hours’ notice for all cancellations. Cancel within 48 hours and pay 50% of the service amount scheduled. Cancel within 24 hours or no-show and pay 100% of the service amount scheduled.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Debit, and cash. Pay-over-time with Affirm may be offered at Vagaro checkout on orders of $50 or more.
