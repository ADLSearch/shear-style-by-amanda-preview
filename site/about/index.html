About Amanda

A San Diego hairstylist creating beautiful, healthy hair in a calm and welcoming environment at Surface Studio in Point Loma.

Book AppointmentGift Cards

Hi, I’m Amanda!

I’m a San Diego hairstylist passionate about creating beautiful, healthy hair in a calm and welcoming environment.

I believe every great appointment starts with listening, so I take the time to understand your goals, lifestyle, and vision before we begin. Whether you’re looking for a precision haircut, a fresh transformation, or simply a refresh, my goal is for you to leave feeling confident, relaxed, and truly cared for.

Every service is tailored to your individual needs, with personalized recommendations and ongoing support to help you maintain your hair long after your appointment.

Building lasting relationships with my clients is one of the most rewarding parts of what I do. I strive to create an experience that’s both relaxing and personalized, so you leave not only loving your hair, but looking forward to your next visit.

What I specialize in

  • Precision haircuts
  • Lived-in color
  • Smoothing treatments
